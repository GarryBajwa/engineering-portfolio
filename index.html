Engineering approach

A machine is one system, not a collection of isolated disciplines.

An alarm may appear in software while the root cause is electrical. A positioning problem may look mechanical but require controls recalibration. A production bottleneck may need a software tool rather than a hardware change.

I start by understanding the complete operating chain, verify assumptions with measurements and observation, isolate the failure, make the smallest practical correction, and validate the result under real operating conditions.

Observe→Measure→Isolate→Correct→Validate
Continuous learning

I’m still learning — and I intend to stay that way.

Every project teaches me something new. Engineering keeps evolving, and no one can know everything. I try to stay curious, question assumptions, learn from mistakes and keep improving how I approach problems.
