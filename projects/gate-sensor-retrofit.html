Sliding Gate Limit Sensor Retrofit

Diagnosed a failed gate-position sensing system and redesigned it from a corrosion-prone mechanical contact arrangement to a glass-sealed reed-switch retrofit with custom 3D-printed mounting hardware.

ControlsSensorsRoot CauseOnshape3D Printing
Field diagnosis at the gate operatorThe repair began by checking the installed drive, wiring and position-sensing path rather than assuming the motor or controller was the cause.

My role

Fault isolation, sensing concept, prototype development, Onshape enclosure design, wiring and integration, positioning, calibration and functional validation.

Engineering objective

Restore reliable gate-position feedback while removing the original corrosion-sensitive mechanical contact as a recurring failure point.

Final sensing concept

Gate Motion→Permanent Magnet→Dual Reed Switches→Existing Controller→Position State
Project overview

An outdoor sliding gate lost reliable position feedback after its original spring-loaded contact sensor deteriorated from moisture and corrosion. I isolated the sensing fault and replaced the vulnerable contact concept with dual glass-sealed reed switches, a permanent magnet and a custom 3D-printed mounting module.

01 · Failure & diagnosis

Finding the weak point instead of replacing the whole system

Observed failure

The original position sensor used a spring-loaded mechanical contact arrangement. Outdoor moisture and corrosion degraded the contact area until reliable switching was lost.

The gate operator therefore had a sensing problem even though the rest of the drive system still required separate verification.

The key decision was to address the sensing architecture that had failed rather than simply reproduce the same exposed mechanical-contact arrangement.

Why the redesign was more robust

Fewer exposed moving contactsThe original spring-loaded contact depended on exposed mechanical contact surfaces that were vulnerable to contamination and corrosion.
Glass-sealed sensing elementsThe replacement uses two reed switches sealed inside glass envelopes, reducing direct exposure of the switching contacts to the outdoor environment.
Controlled magnetic actuationOne permanent magnet positioned between the two reed switches provides the actuation method for the replacement sensing arrangement.
Purpose-built packagingI designed the blue enclosure / bracket in Onshape so the sensing components could be mounted consistently and protected as a serviceable module.

02 · Redesign & integration

From exposed mechanical contact to glass-sealed magnetic sensing

I replaced the failure-prone contact concept with two glass-sealed reed switches actuated by a single permanent magnet, then designed a purpose-built enclosure/bracket in Onshape to position and protect the new sensing hardware.

01

Understand the signal

Confirmed what position feedback the existing controller required and separated the sensing fault from the drive mechanism.

02

Choose a new sensing method

Selected magnetically actuated glass-sealed reed switches to remove the exposed moving electrical contact from the sensing element.

03

Design the mount

Designed the blue enclosure/bracket in Onshape so the two reed switches and magnet could be located consistently and integrated into the existing assembly.

04

Install & calibrate

Wired the replacement, adjusted physical positioning and verified that the controller interpreted the resulting states correctly.

03 · Validation

Verify the complete operating sequence, not only the sensor

Normal stateWith the system ready and no active error, the gate display showed --.
Open commandPressing the remote changed the display to -OP-, confirming that the controller entered the opening sequence.
Mechanical responseThe motor and visible drive gears began rotating and the gate moved as commanded.
System resultNormal gate operation was restored with the new sensing arrangement integrated into the existing controller.

Engineering takeaway

A repair that removes the failure mechanism

Before

Spring-loaded mechanical contact exposed to an outdoor environment, with corrosion and contact degradation creating an unreliable position signal.

After

Two magnetically actuated, glass-sealed reed switches with a permanent magnet and a custom 3D-printed mount — a simpler and more weather-resistant sensing architecture.
